Privacy Policy

Sudoku — RamCraft Labs  |  Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Notice for RamCraft Labs ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our Sudoku mobile application (the "Services").

The Services let you play classic Sudoku across multiple grid sizes (4×4, 6×6, 8×8, 9×9) and difficulty levels, including a daily challenge. You can play entirely without an account, in which case your progress stays only on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, your progress syncs to our servers, and you can appear on a global leaderboard and follow other players.

Questions or concerns? If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. Contact us at ramcraftlabs@gmail.com.

Summary of key points

What do we collect? If you never sign in: nothing leaves your device. If you sign in with Google: your email, display name, and profile picture from Google; your gameplay statistics and scores; and a locally-generated Android device identifier used to merge any guest progress into your new account.

Sensitive personal information? We do not process any.

Minors? Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Do we sell your data? No, never.

Can you delete your data? Yes — the app includes a built-in option to export all your locally stored data and to permanently delete your cloud account data.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

If you never sign in

Your gameplay — puzzle results, statistics, settings, daily challenge streaks, achievements, and favorites — is stored only in your device's local app storage. None of it is sent to our servers. The app generates a local Android device identifier (via your device's Android ID) purely to keep track of your own guest progress on that device; this identifier is not transmitted anywhere while you remain signed out.

If you sign in with Google

When you choose to sign in, we receive the following from Google via our authentication provider (Supabase):

  • Your email address
  • Your name / display name
  • Your Google account profile picture (used as your in-app avatar)

We also sync the following gameplay data to our servers, so your progress is available across devices:

  • Puzzle results: grid size, difficulty, time taken, mistakes, hints used, win/loss outcome, and score earned
  • Your in-progress (unfinished) puzzle, so you can resume it on another device
  • Daily challenge history and streak
  • Achievements unlocked and favorited puzzles
  • Your Android device identifier, sent to our servers at the moment you sign in so that any gameplay completed as a guest on that device can be merged into your new account

Leaderboard & follow data

If you appear on the leaderboard or follow other players, the following becomes visible to other users: your display name, avatar, total/peak score, and games won. Your email address is never shown to other users under any circumstance.

Sensitive information. We do not process sensitive personal information (e.g. racial or ethnic origin, health data, religious beliefs).

Infrastructure-level data. As with any networked app, our hosting and database provider (Supabase) processes your device's IP address as a normal part of handling requests, for security and abuse prevention. We do not separately log or analyze this ourselves.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information only for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and keep your progress synced across your devices.
  • To operate the leaderboard and follow features, showing your display name, avatar, and stats to other users you follow or who follow you.
  • To calculate your in-game score and achievements based on your puzzle results.
  • To merge guest progress into your account when you sign in for the first time on a device you'd previously used without an account.
  • For security and abuse prevention, including on our leaderboard.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

  • Other users (limited). Only your display name, avatar, and score/rank statistics, if you use the leaderboard or follow feature. Never your email address.
  • Service providers. We use Supabase to host our database and handle authentication. Supabase processes your data strictly on our behalf, to operate the Services.
  • Google. If you sign in with Google, authentication is handled by Google per their own terms; we only receive the profile fields described in Section 1.
  • Legal requirements or business transfers, such as if required by law or in connection with a merger or sale of assets.

4. How Do We Handle Google Sign-In?

We offer sign-in via Google only. If you choose this, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture from Google, as described in Section 1. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not control, and are not responsible for, how Google itself handles your information — please review Google's own privacy policy separately.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

Locally-stored data (guest or cached) remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Cloud-stored data (for signed-in users) is retained for as long as you maintain an account. If you delete your account's cloud data (Section 8), we permanently remove it from our active database, except where limited retention is required to prevent fraud or comply with law.

6. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our database enforces row-level access controls so that each account can only read or write its own data. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, and we cannot promise that unauthorized third parties will never defeat these safeguards.

7. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have done so without verified parental consent, we will delete that information promptly. Contact us at ramcraftlabs@gmail.com if you believe this has occurred.

Our follow/leaderboard feature does not support direct messaging between users. Users can only follow one another to compare rankings; a followed user's display name, avatar, and stats are visible — never their email address or other private account details.

8. What Are Your Privacy Rights? (Including Data Export & Deletion)

Export your data. The app includes a built-in option to export all of your locally stored data (statistics, settings, daily challenges, streaks, achievements, favorites) as a JSON file you can save or share at any time.

Delete your data. The app includes a built-in option to permanently delete your account's cloud data from our servers. This removes your statistics, game history, and profile from our database.

Withdraw consent. If we rely on your consent to process your information, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us.

For any other request to review, correct, or delete your personal information, email us at ramcraftlabs@gmail.com.

9. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

No uniform technology standard for recognizing Do-Not-Track (DNT) browser signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to them. If a standard is adopted that we must follow, we will update this notice.

10. Do We Make Updates to This Notice?

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this notice periodically.
